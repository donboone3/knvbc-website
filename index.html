KNVBC PRINCIPLES

Kansas Networks Volleyball Club was created to provide a team-oriented, player-centered opportunity for independent teams based out of Topeka, Kansas to network together and play in the Heart of America region of USA Volleyball.

Relies on the integrity of individual teams; all coaches honor the game and keep players first
Individual teams control their own practices, uniforms, tournament schedules, and team budgets
Only $10 per player club fee with shared volleyballs, equipment, and network support
DOWNLOAD CLUB INFO PACKET

NURTURING EXCELLENCE

Most of the team duties and decisions are up to the individual teams. This results in a variety of team goals and personalities. The strength of the club is our ability to network to help each other reach our individual goals, resulting in more opportunities for kids to play and enjoy youth volleyball at every level.

KSHSAA compliant: max 4 players from the same high school squad per team during school year
Junior Assistant Coach program for experienced high school players mentoring younger teams
Full USAV insurance coverage and sanctioned facility gym request certificates via HOA

REGISTRATION STEPS
WEBPOINT ONLINE
STEP 01
1. HOA / USAV Individual Registration (www.hoavb.org)
Players and coaches register online via Webpoint at www.hoavb.org and select "Ks Networks VBC" as their club ($55 full-season junior/adult; $30 parent member or boys fee).
COACH CERTIFICATION
STEP 02
2. Coach Background Check, IMPACT & SafeSport
Every Head Coach (18+) must complete USAV annual background screening, IMPACT certification, SafeSport certification, and the online JR Coaches Officiating Clinic.
TOPEKA, KS 66619
STEP 03
3. Team Roster & $10/Player KNVBC Fee
Submit the completed Team Info Form, Team Roster Form (6+ registered players), and $10 per player KNVBC club fee ($0 for adult members) to KNVBC, PO Box 19004, Topeka, KS 66619.
FACILITY COVERAGE
STEP 04
4. Practice Gym Insurance Certificate
Request sanctioned facility insurance online through the HOA website at least 2 weeks before practice begins (requires facility owner name, address, and email/fax).

KSHSAA & USAV RULES
KSHSAA ELIGIBILITY
4-PLAYER LIMIT
KSHSAA 4-Player High School Limit
During the school year, no more than 4 players from the same Kansas high school volleyball squad may roster on the same club team.
SCHOOL SEASON RULES
COACHING & SEASON
High School Season & Coaching Restrictions
Athletes cannot participate in club practices/tryouts while their high school volleyball season is active. A player's school volleyball coach cannot coach their club team during the school year.
HOA TOURNAMENTS
WORK TEAMS
Officiating & Work Team Certifications
All teams must certify players and coaches for scorekeeping, R2/down official, and line judging duties to work matches at HOA tournaments.
USAV UNIFORM CODE
JERSEYS 1-99
Uniform & Jersey Requirements
Jerseys require permanent numbers (1-99, no 0 or 00), minimum 4" centered on the chest and 6" centered on the back, with a clearly contrasting Libero jersey.
T.A.P. SOCIAL POLICY
SAFESPORT & KNVBC
T.A.P. STANDARD
T.A.P. Standard (Transparent, Accessible to Parents, Professional)
All electronic communication (texts, emails, social media, team apps) between coaches/adults and minor athletes must be Transparent, Accessible to Parents, and Professional.
COMMUNICATION HOURS
8:00 AM - 9:00 PM
Parent/Guardian Inclusion & Hours
Parents must be copied on all electronic messages to athletes. Coaches may not friend/follow minor athletes from private personal accounts, and routine communications should occur between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM.
